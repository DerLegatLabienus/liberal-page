Organizational Structure — Likud

A national liberal movement — institutions, powers, and election flow

Movement Members
An Israeli citizen aged 17+, resident in Israel, who identifies with the movement's goals. Pays annual dues.
Directly elects the movement chairman and the convention delegates.
Chapter C — Expanded summary: Membership
  • Membership: Israeli citizen aged 17+, resident, identifies with the movement's goals and pays annual dues
  • Right to vote: after 16 consecutive months of membership, may vote in internal elections
  • Right to be elected: after 3 consecutive years of membership, may run for office
  • Termination: the Tribunal may terminate membership for charter violations; the member may appeal within 15 days
  • Movement Honoree: a member over 70 with 25+ years of activity — a special standing

Direct elections
🏛️ Movement Chairman
Directly elected by all movement members • heads the convention and the center
The Likud candidate for Prime Minister of Israel • appoints the oversight committee
Chapter F — Expanded summary: Movement Chairman
  • Direct election: elected by all members in personal, equal and secret elections
  • Candidacy: 500 supporting signatures from members are required to submit a candidacy
  • Two-round system: 40% threshold in the first round; if no candidate passes — a runoff between the top two
  • Oversight committee: supervises the election process, sets rules and timetables
  • Campaign rules: equal access for all candidates, ban on using public funds, full financial transparency

60% by branch affiliation, 40% by eligible voters in the voter rolls
Powers
Sets the charter, elects the Tribunal and auditor, approves policy
Frequency
Once every 4 years, before Knesset or local elections
Chapter E
Founders
Members over 70, active 30+ years — delegates by standing
Chapter E — Expanded summary: The Convention
  • Composition: ~2,500 delegates — 60% by branch affiliation, 40% by voter rolls
  • Frequency: convenes once every 4 years, before Knesset or local-authority elections
  • Supreme authority: sets the charter, elects the Tribunal and internal auditor
  • Founders: members over 70, active 30+ years — automatic delegates by standing
  • Election method: general, direct, equal, secret and proportional

Knesset faction members, branch heads, elected officials. Convenes within 90 days of the convention.

Chapter G
Chapter G — Expanded summary: The Likud Bureau
  • Convening: meets within 90 days of the convention
  • Composition: Knesset faction members, heads of local authorities, branch heads, elected and ex-officio members
  • Management: handles day-to-day matters, subordinate to the Bureau

Chapter H
Chapter H — Expanded summary: The Likud Secretariat
  • Election: elected by the convention in personal elections
  • Records: manages the movement's documents and protocols

The Oversight Committee
Oversees elections
Supervises chairman elections, sets rules, hears appeals, may disqualify candidates.
Chapter F
Chapter F — Expanded summary: The Oversight Committee
  • Appointment: appointed by the Center, chaired by a retired Supreme or District Court judge
  • Composition: up to 7 members; the Center may appoint 2 alternate members
  • Powers: supervises chairman elections — sets rules, hears appeals, may disqualify candidates
  • Decisions: by majority vote, with a casting vote for the presiding judge
  • Finality: decisions are not appealable in the ordinary courts (except the High Court of Justice)

Independent bodies
The Tribunal — Chapter I
Up to 15 judges
President — a retired Supreme or District Court judge. Elected by the convention.
Powers
Termination of membership, reprimand, suspension, fine, expulsion. Final rulings — no appeal to the courts.
Chapter I — Expanded summary: The Tribunal
  • Composition: up to 15 judges, elected by the convention in personal elections
  • President: a retired Supreme or District Court judge
  • Sanctions: warning, reprimand, suspension, removal, fine, expulsion
  • Jurisdiction: internal disputes, termination of membership, disciplinary matters
  • Finality: rulings are final — no appeal to civil courts
  • Costs: may award costs; acts as an authorized arbitrator

The Internal Auditor — Chapter J
Appointment
Elected by the convention, a retired judge. Holds no other position in the movement.
Powers
Financial, operational and branch audits. Sets methods, submits an annual report to the chairman and secretariat.
Chapter J — Expanded summary: The Internal Auditor
  • Appointment: elected by the convention, a retired judge, may not hold another position in the movement
  • Audit: financial and operational audit of all bodies and branches
  • Methods: determines audit methods independently, submits an annual report to the chairman and secretariat
  • Assistants: may appoint advisors and assistants
  • Appeal: findings may be appealed to the Tribunal within 30 days
  • Observer: participates as an observer in Center, Bureau and Secretariat meetings

Local structure
Branches — the local organizational unit (Chapter D)

At least 300 members in a local authority. Its geographic scope is set by the local authority. The movement organization may establish a branch even without 300 members.

Branch council
21-151 members by size. The branch's supreme body.
Branch management
Up to a third of the council, max 51. Day-to-day matters.
Branch secretariat
Ongoing management, oversight, implementing decisions.
Elections committee
5 members, runs local elections, approves voter rolls.
Chapter D — Expanded summary: Branches
  • Minimum: 300 members in a local authority; geographic scope by the authority
  • Branch council: 21-151 members — the branch's supreme body
  • Branch management: up to a third of council members, maximum 51 members
  • Branch secretariat: up to a third of management members, handles day-to-day matters
  • Elections committee: 5 members, runs local elections
  • Dues: annual collection, 10% of revenue transferred to the movement

Special wings
Likud Youth
Members up to age 35. Their own convention, national council, executive committee and chairman.
Chapter L.1
Expanded summary: Likud Youth
  • Age: movement members up to age 35
  • Institutions: convention, national council, executive committee (20 members), chairman
  • Representation: the convention elects 20% of the national council by secret ballot
  • Chairman election: elected directly, two-round system (40% threshold)
  • Representation: presence in all movement bodies; Knesset faction members included

Expanded summary: Local Government
  • Factions: Likud factions in the local-authority councils

🔄 Election flow: Movement members directly elect the movement chairman and the convention delegates. The convention elects the Tribunal, the internal auditor, and the central elections committee. The Center acts between conventions. The Bureau convenes within 90 days of the convention and includes representatives of all bodies.
